    About sensors

    Both sensors are optional. Any standard heart-rate strap works. A smart bike, trainer or power meter works when it uses the standard Bluetooth Fitness Machine or Cycling Power service; a Zone 2 ride then fills main-block watts, cadence and watts at the comparison HR.

    To record in a watch or another app at the same time, turn on that app's option for sharing the strap with a second device, start its recording, then connect here. Keep this app visible during the ride.

    Sensors need an HTTPS page in Android Chrome. The downloaded file supports manual HR. Live samples are processed on this device. Saved ride summaries are included in backups.
