Wellbeing apps

A collection of privacy respecting, simple apps to aid in the betterment of individual wellbeing. Storage either on device or on your choice of S3 bucket.

  • LiftlogLog sets, rest timers and progress.
  • Bike CardioZone 2 and sprint rides by heart rate.
  • TabataInterval timer with large touch controls.
  • Check-inDaily mood and symptom log.

Installation

Open an app, then choose Install in a desktop browser or Add to Home Screen on a phone. Each app installs on its own, with its own icon and offline copy.

Apps on this site share the same S3 storage details.
