Workout apps

Each app keeps its data on this device and works offline after its first visit.

  • LiftlogGym workouts: log sets, time rests, review progress.
  • Bike cardioIndoor cycling with live heart rate, Zone 2 and sprint intervals.
  • TabataA touch-friendly work and rest interval timer.

Install an app

Open an app, then choose Install in a desktop browser or Add to Home Screen on a phone. Each app installs on its own, with its own icon and offline copy.

Apps on this site share one web address, but their data, settings, cloud connections and offline copies stay separate.
